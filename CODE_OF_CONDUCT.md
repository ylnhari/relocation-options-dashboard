# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful project. Participate with empathy, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and contributions rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, sharing another person's sensitive information, or disrupting constructive collaboration.

If conduct needs attention, use the repository's private maintainer contact path. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and the project.
