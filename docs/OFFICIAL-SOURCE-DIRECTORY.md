# Official source directory

**Purpose.** This is a public, primary-source starting point for generic relocation research involving India, Canada/Ontario/Toronto, and the UAE/Abu Dhabi/Dubai. It does not determine an individual's eligibility, tax residence, rent, school place, or cost. Rules and service requirements change; re-open every source and record a fresh as-of date before relying on it.

**Verification.** The links below received best-effort browser and HTTP checks on **2026-08-17** and point to official-government or statutory-authority sources. This is not an uptime assertion: dynamic services, access controls, redirects and later changes can affect access. “Checked” is not a promise that a page, price, programme, or eligibility rule will remain unchanged. Pages identified as a calculator, service card, or law should be preferred over a search-result snippet. No market portals or employer materials are used.

## How to use this directory

- **Official rule** means the requirement, entitlement, procedure, or public rate stated by the named authority. Preserve the source title, page update date (if shown), access date, jurisdiction, and any assumptions.
- **User estimate** means a user-entered amount or model assumption (for example, expected rent, household spending, exchange conversion, commute, or private-school fee). It is not proved by an official source and must be labelled `estimate`, with its own date and method.
- A public rate or fare is still an official rule/input, not a personal result. Recalculate against the live source; do not copy it into a long-lived scenario as a fact.
- “India” and “UAE” are not single city-level rulebooks. Rental, utilities, school admission, transport and health arrangements can be state, emirate, municipality, provider, building, visa, or employment-contract specific.
