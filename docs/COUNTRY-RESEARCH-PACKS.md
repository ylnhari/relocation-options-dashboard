# Country research packs

Use these reusable packs to turn a generic relocation comparison into dated, traceable research. They are deliberately neutral: they identify which official question to answer, not which country, city, school, residence route, employer, or budget is “best.” The underlying public sources are listed in [Official Source Directory](OFFICIAL-SOURCE-DIRECTORY.md).

## Required record for every finding

Capture each research item as:

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Question | The precise rule or operational question, e.g. “Can this permit holder work for a new employer before a new permit is issued?” |
| Jurisdiction | Country plus province/state/emirate/city where relevant |
| Category | One of the categories below |
| Official source | Title and HTTPS URL from the directory, plus the exact service/law subpage used |
| Official rule | Short, conditional paraphrase only; do not turn it into a personal conclusion |
| Checked / source updated | Date opened and the authority’s page-update/effective date where available |
| Applicability gaps | Facts still needed, such as permit type, residence status, child age, school board, building, or employment contract |
| Estimate, if any | Clearly separate user-model input, currency/date/method and uncertainty; never present it as the official rule |

Never put passports, application numbers, addresses, school records, pay statements, account details, family names, or immigration identifiers in a public research record.

## Pack A — India baseline

Use this pack for a destination or continuing-obligations comparison inside India. Several categories are state/UT or local, so record the exact city/state before treating a source as operative.

| Category | Official research question | Starting official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax / payroll | Which tax year, Act/regime, residential status, deductions and payroll contributions apply to the defined income? | Income Tax calculator and EPFO sources in the directory |
| Immigration / work / family / visitors | Which visa or registration category fits the stated purpose and nationality, and what are the dependent/visitor requirements? | Indian Visa Online and Bureau of Immigration |
| Housing / rent | Which enacted state/UT tenancy law, registration and dispute process governs this property? Do **not** assume a model act is operative: tenancy is governed by the respective state/UT rental law. | PIB Model Tenancy Act statement, then the local authority |
| Utilities | Which municipal/provider account, deposit, metering and tenant/owner allocation applies to the actual building? | Relevant city/state utility authority |
| Transit / driving | Which state licensing, registration and transport authority rules apply? | Parivahan plus the local transport authority |
| Childcare / schooling | Which state/board/admission rules and licensed-provider requirements apply? | Ministry of Education RTE materials plus state authority |
| Healthcare | Which public programme, state eligibility rule and provider network apply? | MoHFW plus state health authority |
| Weather / climate | What are the historical normals and seasonal hazards for the exact city, distinct from a travel forecast? | IMD |
| FX | What dated official reference rate will be used for a model conversion, and what spread/fee assumption is separately estimated? | RBI Statistics / published benchmark route |
| Worker / job change | Which central rules are in force and which state rules, contract terms and notice requirements apply? | Ministry of Labour plus state labour authority |
| Retirement / social contribution | Is EPF/EPS coverage triggered for the employment and which current contribution rules/exceptions apply? | EPFO |

## Pack B — Canada / Ontario / Toronto

Use this pack in three layers: federal Canada (immigration, CPP/EI and federal tax), Ontario (tenancy, OHIP, employment, schooling and licensing), then Toronto (transit and municipal utilities). Do not use a Toronto rule as an Ontario-wide rule.

| Category | Official research question | Starting official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax / payroll | What federal and Ontario payroll deductions apply for the selected pay period and employment facts? | CRA payroll deductions; current Ontario payroll tables/calculator |
| Immigration / work / family / visitors | What is the exact permit/visitor category? Are accompanying spouse/common-law partner or children eligible under the current IRCC instructions? | IRCC work permits, family-member eligibility and visitor-visa pages |
| Housing / rent | Is the unit covered by the Residential Tenancies Act and which rent-increase/notice rules apply? What does the signed lease allocate? | Ontario residential rent increases and the current RTA materials |
| Utilities | Which utilities are municipal, condo/builder or separately metered, and who is contractually responsible? | City of Toronto water rates plus the actual lease/condo documents |
| Transit / driving | What are current TTC fare/payment rules and Ontario driver-licence/exchange requirements? | TTC fares and Ontario licensing page |
| Childcare / schooling | What licensed-care rules and local school-board/address/admission requirements apply? Availability and fees require a separate dated estimate. | Ontario child-care and school-finder pages; relevant board |
| Healthcare | Does the person meet current OHIP eligibility and what is the effective date/coverage scope? | Ontario OHIP page |
| Weather / climate | What climate normals apply to the selected station/period, and what current warnings/forecast affect a move date? | Canadian Climate Normals |
| FX | Which Bank of Canada date/rate supports a modelling conversion? Keep actual bank/remittance spread as an estimate. | Bank of Canada exchange rates |
| Worker / job change | Is the permit open or employer-specific; what IRCC process is required before a job/employer change? Separately, what Ontario ESA rights apply? | IRCC change-jobs page and Ontario ESA guide |
| Retirement / social contribution | Which CPP and EI deductions/contributions apply under the current payroll tables, and what plan-specific benefits are separate? | CRA payroll deductions |

**Status caution.** A visitor, approval letter, work permit, physical permit, family member, public-health eligibility and employment relationship each have distinct legal tests. Record the applicable document/status rather than inferring one from another.

## Pack C — UAE / Abu Dhabi / Dubai

Use UAE federal sources for residence, work permits, federal tax and labour rules. Then add the emirate/city layer: Dubai tenancy, utilities, transit, schools and health-insurance arrangements are not interchangeable with Abu Dhabi’s.

| Category | Official research question | Starting official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax / payroll | Is the income employment wage, business activity, VAT-related activity or another category? Which UAE federal tax rule applies, and what foreign-country tax-residence analysis remains outside the UAE source? | FTA natural-person bulletin and VAT service |
| Immigration / work / family / visitors | Which ICP entry/residence service applies, who can sponsor whom, and which document/insurance/financial conditions currently appear for that category? | ICP visa service card and residence pages |
| Housing / rent | Which emirate tenancy registration and rental-dispute/rent-index rules apply to the actual unit? | Dubai Land Department for Dubai; relevant Abu Dhabi authority for Abu Dhabi |
| Utilities | Which provider opens the account, requires a deposit and bills the actual building? | DEWA for Dubai; relevant Abu Dhabi provider/authority for Abu Dhabi |
| Transit / driving | Which emirate authority governs the route, fare product, licensing/exchange and vehicle registration? | RTA for Dubai; Abu Dhabi Mobility for Abu Dhabi |
| Childcare / schooling | Which regulator licenses the provider/school and what are the actual admission, fee, transport and capacity facts? | KHDA for Dubai; ADEK for Abu Dhabi |
| Healthcare | What mandatory/available insurance and health-system rules apply by emirate, sponsor/employer and plan? | DHA insurance system for Dubai; DoH for Abu Dhabi |
| Weather / climate | What official weather, warning and climate evidence applies to the move location/date? | National Centre of Meteorology |
| FX | What dated central-bank/reference source supports a model conversion? Keep conversion-provider fees and rates separate. | Central Bank of the UAE |
| Worker / job change | What work permit is active, what termination/transfer conditions apply, and what must the new employer submit? | MOHRE laws and transfer-work-permit service |
| Retirement / social contribution | Which statutory pension/social-security scheme, if any, covers the worker under nationality/employer/emirate facts? | GPSSA plus the employer’s lawful scheme documents |

## Review gates before a finding becomes a scenario input

1. Confirm that the URL is official, live and specific to the jurisdiction.
2. Record the access date and page’s own effective/updated date; refresh any material item when the decision date changes.
3. Separate the official rule from the facts needed to apply it. If a fact is unavailable, record “not determined,” not a guessed outcome.
4. Label all rents, commute times, utility bills, school fees, take-home pay, FX conversion spreads and household spending as estimates unless an official tariff/calculator directly and currently supports the narrow input.
5. Recheck visa/permit, tax and family eligibility immediately before an application, employment change, signing, payment or travel. Where the outcome is consequential or fact-specific, obtain qualified professional advice.

## Link-validation and maintenance

The directory links were live-checked on **2026-08-17**. Before reuse, open each selected link and update the as-of date. A redirect to a new official URL should be recorded; a blocked authenticated service, CAPTCHA or dynamic selector should be marked “requires interactive verification,” not treated as a dead source. Replace a dead page with the authority’s current page and retain no stale quotation from the old page.
